Sam Knot | Pixie Croix | 28/47

 

There can be a word for anything, so there could be a word for everything. But such words as those, aren't they just labels? Is every real word some kind of an animal? I feel them, winding up and around my body, the shapes and flows of people who don't need names. Thoughts that will never be skinned.
