Sam Knot | Pixie Croix | 22/47

 

A kind of emotional totem pole rises from a cerulean-toothed rage. Start from the bottom, with how you feel, don't try to change it. What else is there? You don't even have to ask. White violets surprise us, pretty little mutants. The chaos of wholeness is peace.
