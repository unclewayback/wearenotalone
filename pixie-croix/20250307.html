Sam Knot | Pixie Croix | 03/47

 

I wake from a dream that I am sure the image will help me remember. I do remember something of it, that I was inside the crown of a tree. I was inside the crown and I knew I was good there, there was something about not needing to travel beyond. Perhaps it was the kind of place you can already see it all from. Which is to say: enough. Pink primula from the flowerbeds behind the house.
