Sam Knot | Pixie Croix | 01/47

 

The Christian Cross, or Crucifix, is one of the most troubling symbols for me. So I thought perhaps something could be gained from working, this Lent, with the figure of it. In lieu of some things I might otherwise do, I would get up every morning - or sit down, perhaps, when I got around to it - and paint something crucifictional.
