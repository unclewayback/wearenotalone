Sam Knot | Pixie Croix | 08/47

 

I find it ugly, what I'm doing. The fish dragons are stupid but at least they're kind of cute. I like the hint of butterfly-wing-edges on the spiral arms, though, I like the thorns dripping blood. That's scarlet, I think. I'm learning about colour. Burnt umber, the brown. And is that grenadine I've paired it with? Ghastly. But enjoyable somehow.
