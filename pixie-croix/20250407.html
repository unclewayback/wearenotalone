Sam Knot | Pixie Croix | 34/47

 

This one reminds me of Blodeuwedd, apart from the fact that it is ugly. But perhaps the ugliness, and the angriness, make sense. Springtime, when the floral scents are strong, it feels like perhaps she is remembering her true nature - but in the black of those same warm quiet nights, when the owls call, it can feel like we still have work to do.
