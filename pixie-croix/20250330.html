Sam Knot | Pixie Croix | 26/47

 

The crackle of electricity, a voice coming over the radio: "This is friendly little labyrinth, broadcasting endless fascination via waves of mild amusement on all channels. Your imponderable distractions are my most industrious pleasures. Hellebore says hello. Please continue branching."
