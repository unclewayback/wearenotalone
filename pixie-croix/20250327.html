Sam Knot | Pixie Croix | 23/47

 

Violet lake, ultramarine, your old friend viridian. You want to dress yourself in it? Go ahead. Monk and monkey, even monkier. We drink to your elf. There are all kinds of habit. I like old ones, patched up with innocence. I know not where they go, only that they tiptoe.
