Sam Knot | Pixie Croix | 02/47

 

The owls appear. I don't know why, I hadn't expected to see them. My thought was simply to see what happened if I let the cross show itself through the negative space. There is a small circular area in the bottom of the lower right section, which I think of as a wound. It started as a circle, I think, then I recall it became a face, an upside down face. Some sort of rude saint who looked a little mean for my liking, so I took the red, vermillion I think it was, and dotted it in all his spaces. With the little red mounds of water still fresh it looked like it was pushing up blood. The cherry tree bare against the evening sky.
