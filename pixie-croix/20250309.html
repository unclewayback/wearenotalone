Sam Knot | Pixie Croix | 05/47

 

This one I couldn't have felt worse about. It took far too long, and mostly in that fixated attitude of trying to correct - or at least improve upon - something you well know is doomed. It's fine, it doesn't matter, it's just a picture. My problem, at least in part, was having an idea of what I wanted to paint before I started. This wasn't meant to be the practise. My mind mocked me, exaggerating: oooh, we gonna makes a dwagon today, shum short of wainbow dwagon.
