Sam Knot | Pixie Croix | 14/47

 

A moose, a rabbit, a silly long-nosed humanoid, an ostrich. Warped echo of the Tetramorph? Moosey Taurus, Leonine boony rairbz, that honking fool Aquarius, Scorpio not about to put her head in the sand? We are blinking such wild cellular splats from the windscreens of our minds in every moment, know it or not.
