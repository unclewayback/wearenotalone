Sam Knot | Pixie Croix | 39/47

 

A little visitation this morning. Lying in bed, veils drawn, soft light filtering through them, careful not to disturb the echoing chamber beyond all room. Hello you. Unfurling astral gymnastics in the stillness, unhoodinghood. The cuckoo begins to call.
