Sam Knot | Pixie Croix | 07/47

 

I take it back to basics, perhaps an exercise at not worrying too much. I'm sure I'm saying something, the marks feel meaningful, there is intent. I am intent, but I don't know why, not the way I might with words. I don't know what these "words" are, just where I want to put them, how they feel. Perhaps knowing why wouldn't be the answer it could seem, just a question in disguise. I begin to see how easy it could be to slap an interpretation on: pick a theme, say individuation, start seeing signs of it everywhere. I can say it has something to do with Equilibrium, though, that's something else.
