Sam Knot | Pixie Croix | 18/47

 

The overall effect reminds me of my puppy looking at me, but she is mostly composed of bunnies. That brainy bunny at the bottom, they're the one imagining all this. Their dream is a kind of cave man - ah yes, of course: the kinder kind of cave man: arty farty ochre fiend! I love viridian, and cadmium orange. How can colours have such delicious names?
